About Recipe Finder

A simple, fast way to discover what to cook next.

Recipe Finder is a lightweight web app that helps you search, browse, and save recipes from all over the world. It pulls real recipe data — ingredients, measurements, cooking instructions, and video links — from TheMealDB, so you always get complete, up-to-date results.
